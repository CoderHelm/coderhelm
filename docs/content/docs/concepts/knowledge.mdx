## Knowledge bases

A **knowledge base** is a set of documents your team uploads: specs, runbooks, policies, product docs. Admins create bases and upload files (PDF, Word, Markdown, text, CSV, Excel and HTML, up to 25 MB each and 500 files per base). Each file shows as uploading, processing, ready, or failed with the reason.

A base can also stay in sync with pages in Notion, Confluence, Google Drive, Sanity, GitHub or a website, or import the knowledge base you already have from an S3 bucket, a Bedrock knowledge base or a Notion or Confluence export. A base can instead be connected in place, searched live with nothing copied: an Amazon Bedrock knowledge base, or any search tool your team runs through its MCP server. See [Knowledge sources](/concepts/knowledge-sources).

A base can be attached to the whole team or to specific agents (planner, coder, reviewer, plan chat). Attached agents get a tool to search it, so a plan can follow your runbook instead of guessing. A base can be marked as a source of truth, and has its own read role (viewers, members or admins), optionally narrowed to only some people or roles.

## The knowledge base page

Each base has one page that answers, in order: is it healthy, does it answer well, what is in it, and who uses it.

- **Status and tiles.** One status for the whole base (**Up to date**, **Syncing (265 of 4,812)**, or how many sources need attention), then four tiles: **Pages searchable** (with links to pages that couldn't be read or were skipped), **Passages**, **Freshness** (the last check, the next one, and whether live updates are on), and **Searched this week** (searches per day, and the change on last week). A problems banner appears only when something is wrong, with each fix, and pages already brought in stay searchable; **See them** filters the content to those pages. Under the header, links jump to Ask, Connections, Content, History, Usage and Access.
- **Ask this knowledge base.** Ask a question and get a cited answer from this base only, through Chat; the thread is saved in your Chat history, marked as asked from the knowledge base page, and **Continue in Chat** opens it. Rate an answer **Helpful** or **Not helpful**, and open what it read and cited. Turn on **Passages only (free: no AI answer)** to see what a search finds instead: the ranked passages, each with a score bar against the best match, whether the last answer cited it (**Cited as S1** or **Not used**), whether it comes from a source of truth, and a link to the original. Passages only uses one embedding and no AI tokens, and people who can't ask in Chat get passages only. Suggested questions show under **Try** and ask at once; they are written from the page titles once and kept until the pages change by more than a tenth, or for a week. You can ask 20 questions an hour from the page. During a first sync, answers use the pages brought in so far, and Ask opens once the first page is searchable.
- **Connections.** One row per source with how it connects: the team's sign-in, a member's own sign-in, a saved API token, the GitHub App, the team's AWS role, public pages, or an uploaded export. Open a row to see whose connection it is and when it was made, its scopes and whether its token still works (for people who manage the base), live updates with the last change event and the next check, what was picked, and what happens if the person who connected it leaves. **Reconnect** signs in again; **Change connection** switches a Notion or Confluence source between the team's sign-in, its saved API token and your own sign-in. **Change pages** picks what it brings in again (**Upload a newer export** for an archive), and the row's menu pauses or removes the source.
- **Content.** Every page as a tree: source, then space or folder, then parent. Filter by state (searchable, reading, couldn't read, skipped, cut short), by when it was updated (7, 30 or 90 days, or older) and by source, or search the titles. Each page says its state in words, when it was updated and indexed, and links to the original. Pages that were left out say why: no text to read, a file type that isn't read, or too large; a page cut short says only its first passages are searchable, so split it to search the rest. Under **Couldn't read** or **Skipped**, people who manage the base choose **Pick pages to read again**, then **Read these again**.
- **Sync history.** Every sync of each source for 90 days, filtered by source: when it started, what started it (the schedule, a change event, a check by hand, adding the source, changed pages, a new sign-in, or reading pages again), how long it took, how it went (**Done**, **Done with problems** or **Stopped**, with why), and what it added, updated, removed, couldn't read or skipped, with how many passages it embedded and kept. Syncs that changed nothing fold into one line; a sync with problems opens to each page, the error and the fix. **Show older syncs** reads further back.
- **Usage.** Searches this week from Chat, agent runs, MCP and API keys, and this page; the most cited pages over 30 days, with how often each was found; the runs and chats that used it; the questions it had no good answer for in 30 days (nothing matched, nothing supported an answer, or rated not helpful), shown with their words, **Add the page** and **Ask again** to the people who manage it, and as counts to everyone else; and the pages no search found in 90 days, which are candidates to leave out.
- **Access.** Who reads it, which agents search it, who sees its pages, and whether it is a source of truth.

A base connected in place (a Bedrock knowledge base or a search tool through MCP) has no tiles, content or history, because nothing is copied: its page shows Ask, then **Connection** (what it is, where it lives, what searches it and how), Usage and Access. **Check connection** runs a test search. See [Knowledge sources](/concepts/knowledge-sources#bedrock-knowledge-bases-connected-in-place).

## Sync limits

There is no fixed cap of 1,000 items. A source keeps up to 50,000 items by default; admins and owners choose from 1,000 to 100,000 under **Sync limits** on the **Knowledge** page, which shows what a full first sync of that size costs. A first sync of more than 2,000 items waits for an admin to press **Start sync**. See [How much a source keeps](/concepts/knowledge-sources#how-much-a-source-keeps).

## Your knowledge in Amazon Bedrock

**Use in Bedrock** on the **Knowledge** page lets Amazon Bedrock agents search your CoderHelm knowledge live through an AgentCore Gateway, or copies it into a Bedrock knowledge base. On a self-hosted install, one button sets up a new Bedrock knowledge base for you. See [Amazon Bedrock](/bedrock).

## Chat

[Chat](/concepts/chat) answers questions from your knowledge and your team's other sources, with a citation on every claim. Viewers can read shared chats, members can ask, and admins manage knowledge.
