title: Roles and permissions
description: The built-in roles, custom roles your team makes, and the capability behind every action.
---

Everyone on a team has one role. Change roles on **Members** (in Settings, under Team); the page also shows the full table of who can do what.

## Built-in roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Viewer** | Read everything on the team; change nothing but their own account. |
| **Member** | Work with CoderHelm: plans, runs, reviews and recommendations. |
| **Admin** | Run the workspace: repos, integrations, agent settings, the token limit and people. |
| **Owner** | Everything, plus account-level changes and managing admins. |

## Capabilities

Every request to CoderHelm is checked in one place, on the server, against the capability its action belongs to. Each capability has a lowest built-in role that holds it.

| Lowest role | Capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| Viewer | `view`, `view_team`, `own_account` |
| Member | `plans`, `runs`, `reviews`, `recommendations`, `view_alerts`, `view_integrations` |
| Admin | `people`, `roles`, `delete_plans`, `repos`, `agent_settings`, `memory`, `integrations`, `data_sources` (includes team tools and skills), `tests`, `alerts`, `model_provider`, `system`, `budget`, `support` |
| Owner | `manage_admins`, `team_name`, `disconnect` (removes Jira or the model key), `reset` |

A request without the capability it needs gets `403`, with the name of the missing capability.

## Custom roles

When the built-in roles don't fit, make your own. For example, a "Release manager" who is a member but can also change reviewer and repository settings, or an "Analyst" who can read everything and manage data sources.

1. On **Members**, under **Custom roles**, click to create a role.
2. Give it a name and a description, start from Viewer, Member or Admin, and tick the capabilities it should hold. New capabilities CoderHelm adds later appear in the editor by themselves.
3. Give the role to people from the role selector, or pick it when you invite someone.

The rules, enforced on the server:

- Only built-in admins and owners create, edit, delete or give custom roles. A custom role can never manage roles itself.
- Owner-only capabilities (account changes, managing admins and owners, renaming the team, reset, disconnecting) and `support` are never offered in a custom role.
- Nobody can create, edit or give a role that holds more than they hold, or change someone whose role holds more than theirs.
- Deleting a role that people hold asks which role they move to.
- Every role created, edited, deleted or given out is recorded in **Role changes**, kept for a year.

Everyone else on the team can see the custom roles and what each one can do. A change to a role applies within 30 seconds.

## Invites and approvals

Invites expire after 30 days; admins can resend them.

Some actions need a second person, even an admin: approving a new version of a [team tool](/concepts/skills#team-tools) needs another admin when the team has more than one.
