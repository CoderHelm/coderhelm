**Chat** answers questions about your team's work from your own sources: your code (it reads your connected repositories itself), [knowledge](/concepts/knowledge), connections, memories, data sources and skills. Open it from **Chat** in the sidebar, or press ⌘K and pick **New chat**.

## Asking

The page opens on the question box, ready to type. Under it are a few suggested questions built from your team's own repositories, recent runs and knowledge.

- **Chat decides what to read.** Every question can use everything you can see: all your connected repositories and the knowledge open to your role. Name a repository in the question ("How is web put together?") and Chat reads that one.
- **Type @** (or press the **@** button) to point a question at one repository or knowledge collection. It shows as a chip in the box; remove the chip to undo it. Nothing is narrowed unless you choose it.
- **Attach a file** to a question with the paperclip, or drop it on the box. Up to three files per question.
- **Enter** sends, **Shift+Enter** starts a new line, **Esc** stops an answer while it is coming in, and **⌘/** (Ctrl+/ on Windows and Linux) starts a new chat.

## How Chat researches

Chat works like a teammate who opens the code: it looks a repository over (its README, the top of its folders, its manifests such as `package.json` or `Cargo.toml`, and agent notes such as `AGENTS.md`), lists folders, searches the code, reads files by line range and checks the code graph, over as many steps as the question needs. Every read is pinned to one commit, so a citation shows exactly the lines it quotes. Chat never asks you to open or paste files its tools can read; an answer that does is sent back to the tools before you see it.

## Answers

- What Chat did shows as one quiet line above the answer ("Read 4 files in web, searched the code"). Open it to see each step.
- Every claim has a small number. Hover or tap it to see the source: the file's path and lines, with a link to them on GitHub at that commit, or the knowledge page's title and date.
- The sources are listed under the answer, each once by its real name. Open one to read what was quoted.
- When nothing in your sources supports an answer, it says so instead of making one up.
- Under each answer: **Copy** (the text without the citation numbers), **Ask again** on the latest answer, and a thumbs up or down. Ratings help tune what Chat reads.

## Chats

Your chats are listed on the left, newest first, grouped by day, with a search box. On a phone the list opens from the button at the top left. Each of your chats has a menu to rename it, share it with the team or delete it.

A chat is private until you share it. Teammates can then read it, but only you ask in it.

## Who can do what

Everyone on the team can read chats shared with them. Members can ask, rate and manage their own chats. Admins manage knowledge. A custom role follows the same capabilities: **Ask questions in Chat** and **Read knowledge open to their role**.

Chat's model calls are metered under their own name on the usage page, and count toward your [token limits](/concepts/token-limits).

Bookmarks to the old address keep working: links to the old chat page open Chat.
