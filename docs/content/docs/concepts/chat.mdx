This feature is being built and is not on main yet. This page describes it as it is being built and will change before it ships.

**Chat** answers questions from your team's own sources: [knowledge](/concepts/knowledge), connections, repositories and the code graph, memories, data sources and skills. Open it from **Chat** in the sidebar, or press ⌘K and pick **New chat**.

## Asking

The page opens on the question box, ready to type. Under the box are a few suggested questions built from your team's own data: your knowledge, your repositories and your recent runs. Picking one asks it with the sources it needs.

- **Sources** sit inside the box as chips. Open **Sources** to turn kinds of sources on or off, pick which knowledge to read, and choose the repository for code, memories and skills. Remove a chip to turn that source off. Chat remembers your picks for next time.
- **Attach a file** to a question with the paperclip, or drop it on the box. Up to three files per question.
- **Enter** sends, **Shift+Enter** starts a new line, **Esc** stops an answer while it is coming in, and **⌘/** (Ctrl+/ on Windows and Linux) starts a new chat.

## Answers

- The answer streams in as it is written. What Chat looked up shows above it, folded once the answer is done.
- Every claim has a numbered citation. Click the number, or the source card under the answer, to read the passage it rests on, where it comes from and how current it is.
- When nothing in the chosen sources supports an answer, it says so ("Not in your sources") instead of making one up.
- Under each answer: **Copy** (the text without the citation numbers), **Ask again** on the latest answer, and a thumbs up or down. Ratings help tune what Chat reads.

## Chats

Your chats are listed on the left, newest first, grouped by day, with a search box. On a phone the list opens from the button at the top left. Each of your chats has a menu to rename it, share it with the team or delete it.

A chat is private until you share it. Teammates can then read it, but only you ask in it.

## Who can do what

Everyone on the team can read chats shared with them. Members can ask, rate and manage their own chats. Admins manage knowledge. A custom role follows the same capabilities: **Ask questions in Chat** and **Read knowledge open to their role**.

Chat's model calls are metered under their own name on the usage page, and count toward your [token limits](/concepts/token-limits).

Bookmarks to the old address keep working: links to the old chat page open Chat.
